About

Built by the people who actually trade it.

Since 2019

Small start, sharp focus.

ZeroPlus Derivatives got its start in 2019, when a small opportunity in options turned into a strategy worth building on. Since then, that single idea has grown into a full book of strategies across a wide range of products.

Markets don't stand still, so we don't do hierarchy. Every trader, developer, and analyst who joins us gets a real say in how we trade and how we build — from their first week onward.

Our Approach

Data first. Ego last.

Feelings don't belong in a trade, so we design them out. Our approach leans on data, not conviction, to add liquidity and keep our process transparent — across all 18 US options exchanges, on software we wrote ourselves, running both programmatically and by hand.

Our Edge

None of this works without all three.

Built In-House

We write our own software so we're never waiting on someone else's roadmap. Markets change fast — our tools have to keep up.

Flat by Design

The best fix for a hard problem rarely comes from the top down. We built a desk where anyone can raise their hand.

Our Own Money

We don't trade with anyone else's capital. That keeps our incentives simple: make the strategy work, or don't run it.

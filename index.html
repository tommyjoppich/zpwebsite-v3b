How We Operate

Three principles run through everything we trade.

Built In-House

Every tool we trade with is ours — designed, written, and tuned by the people who use it every day. Nothing off the shelf, nothing licensed out.

Flat by Design

Good ideas shouldn't need a chain of approvals. Traders and engineers sit together and decide together.

Our Own Money

Every strategy is funded with our own capital. When a trade works, it's ours. When it doesn't, that's ours too.

Who We Are
We put engineers next to traders, hand them real ownership, and get out of the way — no titles, no turf, just people building something that has to work every single day.
